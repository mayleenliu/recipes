# [Velvety Pumpkin Pasta with Spicy Sage & Nut Crumble](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdeGvacRMab/)

![[pumpkin-pasta-sage-nut.png|483]]

## Ingredients
Spicy Sage & Nut Crumble  
- [ ] ½ cup pecans, roughly chopped  
- [ ] ½ cup walnuts, roughly chopped  
- [ ] 1 tbsp finely chopped garlic  
- [ ] 10–15 fresh sage leaves  
- [ ] 1–2 tbsp fresh thyme leaves  
- [ ] 1 tsp red pepper flakes  
- [ ] Flaky salt, to taste  
- [ ] Olive oil, for frying and toasting  
  
Pumpkin Pasta  
- [ ] 225 g rigatoni  
- [ ] ½ cup shallot, finely chopped  
- [ ] ½–1 tsp red pepper flakes, to taste  
- [ ] 1½ tbsp tomato paste  
- [ ] 1¼ cups pumpkin purée  
- [ ] ½ cup water (to thin, and later add reserved pasta water)  
- [ ] ⅓ cup cream (I used a combination of half-and-half and heavy cream)  
- [ ] ½ cup finely shredded Parmesan, plus more for serving  
- [ ] 1 cup reserved pasta water  
- [ ] Bouillon, according to package direction  
- [ ] 1 tbsp Butter  
- [ ] 1 tsp garlic powder  
- [ ] Salt, to taste

## Instructions
- [ ] Fry sage in olive oil until crisp; set aside. In the same pan, cook garlic until golden, then add pecans, walnuts, thyme, chili flakes and flaky salt. Toast until fragrant, crumble the sage back in and set aside.  
- [ ] Sauté shallot until soft. Add chili flakes and tomato paste and cook 1 minute. Stir in pumpkin, 1/2 water and cream until smooth and glossy. Add 1 tsp garlic powder. For an extra-silky sauce, blend until smooth.  
- [ ] Cook rigatoni al dente in well-salted water, reserving 1 cup pasta water. Stir butter and bouillon into the reserved water. Add pasta to the pumpkin sauce with about ½ cup bouillon pasta water, adding more as needed. Toss in Parmesan until glossy and creamy.  
- [ ] Plate and top generously with the spicy sage and nut crumble.
